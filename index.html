What it is

A movie app built for the part you already do with friends.

01

Rate and log

Score films on a straight 1–10 scale and keep a running record of everything you’ve watched — plus a watchlist for the ones you keep meaning to get to.

02

Follow your friends

A feed of what the people you know are watching and rating, with reviews and comments so you can argue about a 6 that should have been an 8.

03

Find the next one

Recommendations that come from your friends’ taste rather than a studio’s marketing budget — and a note of where each film is streaming.
